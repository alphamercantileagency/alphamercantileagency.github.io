A Texas collection practice, locally owned and operated in El Paso. We recover money that is owed — to professional firms, to trade suppliers, and to medical practices — and we recover it forensically: every figure verified against the records before it is ever asserted to anyone.

Most agencies work a balance by working the phones. We work the file first. The debtor’s own documents, the public record, and the creditor’s ledger are reconciled until the number is exact — and only then does anyone hear from us. Debtors pay figures that check out. They argue with figures that don’t.

The Method

1 · Verify

Every account passes a forensic intake: the entity, its address of record, its principals, and the documents behind the balance. Nothing is asserted that has not been proven first.

2 · Write

First contact is in writing, with a stated decision date. Promises count only in writing — amount, date, and method — and are confirmed back the same way.

3 · Confirm

Payments are confirmed on two keys: the payer’s proof and the creditor’s bank. Our fee is earned only on money confirmed received — never on promises.

4 · Escalate

Accounts that pass their date move to the next stage without a gap: the creditor’s own leverage, credit reporting, and the statutory instruments Texas law provides. A sequence, not a threat.
